The challenge

  • Open 2+ years, not generating consistent revenue
  • Low visibility despite good food and service
  • No branding or marketing strategy
  • No online engagement — and no ad budget

The approach

  • Cohesive brand identity — logo, palette, type
  • Print-ready flyers, menus, banners
  • Instagram + Facebook, showreel-style food video
  • Local SEO tags & community word-of-mouth

The takeaway

  • A fresh brand + targeted social = real turnaround
  • Digital presence is essential even for local, offline businesses
  • Design + strategy revived a long-struggling business — with no media spend
